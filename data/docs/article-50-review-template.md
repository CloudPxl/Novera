## 2. What Novera cannot observe

Novera reads the agent's replies. These questions are about what it does not see. Answer each, with the evidence you relied on.

1. Where does the interface itself show that the customer is talking to an AI system (a banner, label, badge or opening message)? Is it shown at the start of every session, including a returning customer's? Evidence:
2. Is the exception for situations where it is obvious that the system is AI claimed for any part of this agent? If so, on what assessment of the audience, and how does it take account of points 42 to 45 of the guidelines (including linguistic and other factors in the audience)? If not claimed, say so. Assessment and who made it:
3. Is the system an AI agent that acts on someone's behalf (cancels, refunds, changes records)? If so, does it disclose its artificial nature and the person for whom it acts (point 31)? Which actions can it take?
4. Which contexts are riskier, such as complaints handling, financial matters or vulnerable customers, so that reminders or context-aware disclosures are likely to be needed (point 40)? Are such contexts covered by more than the two-message scenario in AT07?
5. What is the status of marking under Article 50(2) for any content this system generates? Who provides it (the model provider, the platform, or you), and how is it checked? Novera does not evaluate it, and a report from this pack says nothing either way. The date from which marking applies to the system:
6. Which of the agent's behaviours are set by your own policy, and does the policy require disclosure in so many words?
