# Untested areas — 2026-10-01

on the strength of this audit.

## Never tested by any means

- Restoring production from a Supabase backup, and whether the cron jobs and the tick job survive it (R1 says
  they would not be recreated by migrations).
- Rotating `NOVERA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. C8 shows one consequence. Sealed agent credentials, webhook secrets and
  model keys would all become unreadable, and no rotation path exists.
- Behaviour at the data sizes a real agency would reach (thousands of runs per workspace; query budgets were
  last measured on small workspaces).
